# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in SpireAgent welcoming and harassment-free,
regardless of background, identity, appearance, experience or ability. We act
with empathy, respect differing technical views, accept constructive feedback
and focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling,
personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, or other
conduct inappropriate in a professional open-source project.

## Responsible AI Play

Use AI play for research, personal runs or informed private groups. Avoid
misleading public multiplayer, leaderboards, events or viewers about AI
participation. Disclose AI involvement when sharing runs.

## Scope And Enforcement

This Code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions and
other project-hosted spaces. Maintainers may remove content or participation
that violates it. Report abusive or harassing behavior privately to
`jiyikun2002@gmail.com`; maintainers will protect reporter privacy and respond
proportionately, from correction through permanent exclusion.

This policy is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
